ABOUT US

About the Association

"Brainova" is the student association of the CSE(Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning) Department, established to foster innovation and collaboration in AI and Machine Learning. It was founded and inaugurated by Mr.S.Mohammed Jaleel (Founder & Chairman) on September 18th, 2024, in the presence of administrative members, with the support of our HOD and faculty members. Brainova aims to empower students through tech events, workshops, and symposiums, creating a dynamic platform for learning, research, and skill development in emerging technologies.

About the Symposium

Neuraverse 2k26 is more than a symposium; it's a gateway to the future. After the massive success of Neuraverse '25, we are back with grander challenges, higher prize pools, and cutting-edge tech discussions. Join us for an action-packed day of innovation and collaboration.

About the Department

The Department of Computer Science and Engineering (Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning) was established in 2022 with a vision to excel in AI-driven education and research. Led by Dr. B. Guru Prakash, Head of the Department, we offer a cutting-edge curriculum that integrates core computer science with advanced AI and ML technologies. Our focus is on hands-on learning, industry collaborations, and research in areas like deep learning, data science, and robotics, With experienced faculty and state-of-the-art labs, we prepare students for dynamic careers in AI. We actively engage in workshops, hackathons, and innovative projects, shaping the future of intelligent computing.
